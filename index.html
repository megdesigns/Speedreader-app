📖 Speed Reader

Hi! I'm someone who loves reading but found myself struggling to keep up with all the books I wanted to dive into. Between work, life, and everything in between, I needed a way to read more in less time.

That's when I discovered speed reading. I built this tool to help me (and now you!) read faster while staying focused. The current word lights up as you read, keeping your eyes moving smoothly through the text at your own comfortable pace.

Start at 350 words per minute, or adjust to whatever feels right. The goal isn't just speed—it's actually finishing the books you've been meaning to read.
