Hi! I'm MEG, and I'm mostly a Product Designer (seriously considering calling myself a Design Engineer because why not). Anyway. Today I was scrolling through Twitter and came across a tweet that has reached 14M impressions as I type this. It was a speed reading challenge and… of course this started because of Twitter.

People were in the comments testing themselves, some were asking if someone can build an app that does this, and you may not believe me when I say this — some other people in the comments were already replying with what they built, in literal minutes. I was intrigued, ngl. I’ve been trying to read more, but between life and a short attention span (sometimes), I don’t always make time to finish books the way I want. So I thought: if people can build something like this that fast, I can probably build one for myself. This tool highlights each word to keep you focused while you read at your own pace.

I also plugged in one of my Apple Music playlists for the weirdos that listen to music while they read. It’s just instrumentals though. Start at a comfortable speed, then ramp it up when you’re ready. It’s less about “being fast” and more about actually finishing what you start. Enjoy <3
